# About Stranger Mingle

Stranger Mingle is a brand of **Salty Media Production (opc) Pvt Ltd**. We are built to solve a problem millions of Indians face: feeling lonely in a crowded city. Making friends after college shouldn't be this hard. We create safe, structured social spaces where strangers meet and genuine friendships begin — not online, but in real life.

Stranger Mingle is **NOT** a dating app, matrimonial service, or networking platform. It is exclusively focused on platonic friendships. All events are in-person (plus periodic online intercity meetups), held in public venues, facilitated by verified and trained hosts, and governed by a strict zero-harassment policy.

---

## Our Mission

Our mission is to help adults make real connections — primarily through in-person events, with online meetups and sessions as well. We create safe spaces where strangers meet and friendships begin through organised weekend events and virtual gatherings across Indian cities.

Whether you're new in town, an introvert, or simply don't know how to make new friends as an adult, we are here to help. Our mission goes beyond just making friends; we aim to improve your quality of life through meaningful events, a disciplined approach, and a safe, respectful environment.

## How We Mingle (Event Categories)

We organize diverse weekend activities to cater to different interests and social comfort levels:

*   **Social Meetups:** Chai circles, coffee gatherings, and board game nights for casual conversations in low-pressure settings.
*   **Adventures:** Trekking excursions, nature walks, and cycling trails to bond over shared physical experiences.
*   **Culture:** Heritage walks, art gallery visits, and food tours to explore your city alongside others.
*   **Volunteering:** Animal shelter volunteering, community cleanups, and social service drives to give back while building connections.
*   **Online Events:** Structured virtual meetups, online multiplayer games, and topic-based sessions to mingle from anywhere in the country.

## How It Works

Building adult friendships shouldn't feel intimidating. We structure our experience into four simple stages:

1.  **Browse Events:** Check the events page on our platform to see what's happening in your city or online. First-timers often start with relaxed formats like a Sunday morning coffee circle or a virtual ice-breaking session.
2.  **Login & Book:** Join our member community and book your spot. Every member completes a basic verification process (including name, age, phone, and email verification) to ensure safety and accountability.
3.  **Show Up:** Most attendees (about 80%) show up alone, so you will be in great company. Our trained hosts lead the first 30 minutes with ice-breakers and guided introductions so no one feels left out.
4.  **Connect:** Exchange contact details, join member chats, play online games, and plan future catch-ups. Friendships take time, but they start by showing up.

## Community Standards: Built for India

Stranger Mingle is dedicated to Indian communities. In India, people often carry unnecessary social baggage regarding class, caste, gender, religion, skin color, or college pedigree. These elements create invisible walls that make organic socializing difficult.

We dismantle these walls. We judge people solely by how they treat others, their values, and their character — not by their last name, educational pedigree, or bank balance.

*   **Anti-Discrimination:** Strict guidelines prohibit discrimination based on caste, religion, gender, skin color, class, or educational background.
*   **Respect-First Policy:** All members must interact with mutual respect.
*   **Accountability:** Immediate removal and permanent ban for creating unsafe or exclusionary environments.

## Member Interactions & Personal Responsibility

Initial connections are facilitated in safe, structured environments during organized events (in person or online). Members are free to interact directly with each other outside of organized events, exchange contact details, meet for coffee, or develop relationships. 

These private interactions are the member's personal choice and responsibility. Stranger Mingle does not take responsibility for interactions, relationships, or incidents occurring outside official group activities or event premises.

## Maintaining Quality & Sustainability

Stranger Mingle is built to be a sustainable, high-quality community. While we value accessibility, maintaining high-quality standards requires resources. Event venues need to be pre-screened, safety measures must be funded, and trained organizers require support. 

We charge reasonable fees (typically ₹299 to ₹599 for casual events) purely to maintain the platform, ensure events run smoothly, and deliver a reliable experience weekend after weekend.
