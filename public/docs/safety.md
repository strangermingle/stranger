# Safety Guidelines at Stranger Mingle

Stranger Mingle is one of India's safest social communities — particularly for women, girls, and people of all genders. Safety is our foundation, not just a feature. 

---

## 1. Before Attending Events

*   **Complete Registration Honestly:** Provide accurate personal information during registration. Use your real name, age, phone number, and email. Complete verification honestly, as this protects the entire community.
*   **Inform Someone You Trust:** Tell a friend or family member where you're going. Share the venue address, event time, and expected return time. Keep your phone fully charged.
*   **Review Event Details:** Read the full event description, location, and requirements beforehand. Note the organizer's contact details.
*   **Physical Preparedness:** Ensure you are physically fit for active events (like treks or cycling). Disclose any medical or health conditions to organizers beforehand and bring necessary medications. Do not participate if you are feeling unwell.

---

## 2. During Events: Personal Safety

*   **Stay in Public Venues:** All Stranger Mingle events are held exclusively in pre-screened public spaces. Do not leave the event venue with someone you just met. If you need to step out, inform the organizer.
*   **Trust Your Instincts:** If someone or something makes you uncomfortable, trust your gut feeling. You have the right to decline conversations or move away from anyone at any time. Inform the event host immediately.
*   **Protect Personal Information:** You are never obligated to share details beyond your first name. Be cautious about sharing your home address, workplace, daily routine, or social media handles.
*   **Manage Belongings:** Keep your valuables secure. Do not leave bags, wallets, or phones unattended.
*   **Alcohol & Substance Safety:** Do not arrive at events intoxicated. Consume alcohol responsibly if the event permits it, never leave your drink unattended, and never accept drinks from strangers. Illegal substances are strictly prohibited.

---

## 3. Specific Safety Guidelines for Women

We are committed to maintaining a safe, respectful environment for women. Women can attend alone or in groups with complete confidence.

*   **Your Rights:**
    *   The right to participate freely without fear of harassment or advances.
    *   The right to say "no" to any interaction, conversation, or contact request.
    *   The right to be treated with absolute dignity.
    *   The right to immediately report anyone making you uncomfortable.
*   **Additional Precautions:**
    *   Attend your first few events during daytime hours if possible.
    *   Arrange your own transportation instead of relying on rides from people you just met.
    *   Share your live location with a trusted friend/family member during the event.
    *   If you choose to meet a member one-on-one later, always select busy public locations.
*   **Immediate Action for Harassment:**
    If anyone makes unwanted advances, inappropriate comments, or touches you without consent:
    1.  Clearly and firmly tell them to stop.
    2.  Inform the event organizer/host immediately.
    3.  Move to a different area or closer to the host.
    4.  Leave the venue if you feel unsafe (we will provide a full refund).
    5.  Submit a formal report to the safety team.

## 4. Strictly Prohibited Behavior

The following behaviors will result in **immediate removal** from events and a **permanent, irreversible ban** from the platform without warning or appeal:

### Harassment & Misconduct
*   Sexual harassment of any kind.
*   Unwanted physical contact or touching.
*   Inappropriate sexual comments or advances.
*   Following or stalking members during or after events.
*   Taking photos or videos of others without consent.
*   Sending unsolicited explicit messages or images.

### Discrimination
*   Discrimination based on caste, religion, gender, skin color, class, or background.
*   Derogatory remarks about someone's education, income, or profession.
*   Exclusionary behavior or forming "elite" cliques.

### Threatening Behavior
*   Physical violence or threats of violence.
*   Verbal abuse, bullying, or intimidation.
*   Aggressive body language, hate speech, or harassment.

### Misuse of Platform
*   Using events for dating, romantic matchmaking, hookups, or unsolicited flirting.
*   Promoting or selling products/services to members.
*   Recruiting for multi-level marketing (MLM) schemes or business opportunities.
*   Collecting member information for unauthorized or commercial purposes.

### Illegal Activities
*   Drug possession, use, or distribution.
*   Theft or property damage.
*   Any activity violating Indian law.

---

## 5. After Events: Safe Interactions

*   **Meeting Outside Events:** Meeting people you connected with during events is at your own risk and responsibility. We recommend meeting in busy public places during daytime, in groups rather than one-on-one initially, and arranging your own transportation.
*   **Digital Communication Safety:** You are not obligated to stay in touch with everyone. Block anyone who makes you uncomfortable on WhatsApp or social media. Be cautious of members who immediately pressure you to move to private channels.
*   **Financial Safety:** Never lend money to people you just met. Be highly skeptical of sob stories, urgent financial requests, or investment schemes. Never share bank details, OTPs, or credit card info.

---

## 6. Emergency Numbers (India)

If you experience or witness an emergency:
*   **Immediate Danger / Police:** Call **100** or emergency response **112**.
*   **Medical Emergency:** Call ambulance **108** and notify the event host.
*   **Women's Helpline:** Call **1091**.

---

## 7. Reporting Violations & Concerns

We investigate all reports thoroughly and confidentially. Your identity will be protected.

*   **What to Report:** Harassment, guideline violations, fake identities, safety concerns, or anything making you feel unsafe.
*   **How to Report:**
    *   *During Events:* Inform the host immediately.
    *   *After Events:* Email **strangermingleteam@gmail.com** or use the website "Report Concern" form.
*   **What to Include:** Event name/date, names/descriptions of individuals involved, detailed description of the incident, and any screenshots/photos if relevant.

---

## 8. Host & Organizer Responsibilities

Our verified and trained hosts are committed to your safety:
*   They verify registrations before events.
*   They brief participants on safety guidelines.
*   They monitor group interactions and intervene immediately if they notice inappropriate behavior.
*   They are fully authorized to remove anyone violating guidelines without refund.
