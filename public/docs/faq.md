# Frequently Asked Questions

This FAQ provides answers to common questions about joining Stranger Mingle, attending weekend friendship events, safety, payments, and community standards in Indian cities.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### What is Stranger Mingle and how does it work?
Stranger Mingle is a community platform that helps adults make real friends through organized weekend events across Indian cities. We host coffee meetups, board game nights, treks, cultural walks, and volunteering drives. Simply browse our events, register for ones that interest you, and show up. Our trained hosts handle all introductions and ice-breakers so that you don't have to worry about awkward small talk.

### Is Stranger Mingle free to use?
No, but we keep fees highly reasonable (typically ₹299–₹599 per event). We charge purely to cover venue bookings, safety measures, host support, and platform maintenance. This ensures quality experiences and sustainable, reliable operations.

### Do I need to create an account?
Yes. You need to register on our Member Portal and complete a basic verification process (name, age, phone number, and email). This is a mandatory safety measure to keep bad actors out and ensure everyone feels secure.

### Which cities do you operate in?
Our primary hub is Pune, and we are expanding to Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi, Hyderabad, and other major Indian cities. Check the events page to see what is scheduled in your location.

### Who joins Stranger Mingle?
Our community consists of adults who want to expand their social circle: WFH professionals, IT engineers, people new to a city, students, and introverts. We judge members by their character and values, not by their last name, educational pedigree, or bank balance.

## 2. Events & Registration

### How do I register for an event?
Go to our Events page, browse the upcoming schedule, select your event, and click "Join / Book". You will be guided to our secure payment gateway. You'll receive a confirmation email with venue details and host info once the booking is complete.

### How many people attend each event?
We keep group sizes small — typically 15 to 25 people. This prevents crowded chaos and makes it easy for everyone to have actual conversations and connect.

### Can I bring a friend?
Yes, but they must register separately with their own account, complete verification, and pay the event fee. Most people attend alone, so do not worry if you are coming by yourself!

### Can introverts join?
Absolutely. Many of our members are introverts. Our events are structured specifically to be low-pressure. We guide the first 30 minutes with ice-breakers and small-group arrangements so that no one has to force a conversation or stand alone.

## 3. Safety & Community Rules

### Is Stranger Mingle safe for women and girls?
Yes. Women's safety is our highest priority. We enforce a strict zero-tolerance policy against harassment, verify all registrations, hold events exclusively in busy public venues, and train hosts in safety management. Any harassment results in an immediate and permanent ban.

### What is your policy on harassment?
We have absolute zero tolerance. Verbal, physical, or digital harassment, unwanted physical contact, or stalker-like behavior results in immediate removal and a permanent platform ban without warnings or appeals.

### Do you tolerate discrimination?
Absolutely not. We stand firmly against discrimination based on caste, religion, gender, skin color, economic status, or educational background. Anyone who discriminates or acts in an exclusionary manner will be banned permanently.

## 4. Payments, Cancellations & Refunds

### What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and major digital wallets through our encrypted payment gateway.

### Can I get a refund if I cannot attend?
Yes, depending on when you cancel:
*   **48+ Hours Notice:** 100% refund or platform credit.
*   **24–48 Hours Notice:** 50% refund or 75% platform credit.
*   **Less than 24 Hours:** Tickets are non-refundable, but you can transfer your ticket to a verified friend by notifying us.

### What happens if Stranger Mingle cancels the event?
If we cancel due to weather, venue issues, or low registration, you will receive a 100% refund or a 110% platform credit (including a 10% bonus for the inconvenience).

## 5. After Events & Platform Conduct

### Is Stranger Mingle a dating app?
No. Stranger Mingle is strictly for platonic friendships. We do not facilitate dating, romantic matchmaking, or hookups. Using events or community chats for romantic pursuit is prohibited and can lead to account suspension.

### Can I meet members outside official events?
Yes. Members often exchange contact details and meet up independently. However, these interactions are your personal choice and responsibility. We recommend meeting in busy public locations initially and taking standard safety precautions.
